Terms of Service

Please read carefully before commissioning. By requesting a commission, you agree to the following terms.

Painterly anime and furry character art, illustration, and project-based artwork. Commissions start at $60 USD.

Pricing can be sensitive to complexity, character count, background detail, usage, and deadline.


What I Offer

  • Character art
  • Portraits / busts
  • Full-body illustrations
  • Character sheets
  • Promotional or project artwork
  • Dark, surreal, emotional, or cinematic focused pieces

Pricing

  • Base price: $60 USD
  • Larger or more complex work is quoted individually.
  • Commercial use, multiple characters, detailed backgrounds, tight deadlines, or complex designs may increase the price.

Process

  1. Send a request through the form.
  2. I review the idea and give a quote.
  3. 50% Payment is confirmed.
  4. I send progress/sketch updates.
  5. A Preview of final work is sent.
  6. Other 50% Payment is confirmed.
  7. Final artwork is delivered as a high-resolution file.

Do's & Don'ts

DO's: Small characters, Furries, Men, Femboys, Women, Very old people, OCs, Personas / self insert, Fanart (depending on the copyright holder), Very muscular men or men with large amounts of facial hair, NSFW (discuss DMs).

DON'Ts: Mecha / full armor, Any form of NFT, Small characters/childlike characters in NSFW settings.


Payment

  • Full payment required before starting the commission.
  • All prices are in American dollars (USD), you can use Wise conversion calculator to estimate the price in your currency.
  • Backgrounds can come at an additional price depending on complexity.
  • If wanting to purchase commercial rights, please discuss with me directly.
  • Any additional characters after the first are at full price (example: knee up commission with two characters 120+60 vs knee up commission with 3 characters 120+60+120).
  • Character prices are based on how they appear in frame (example: one character is half body and 2 other characters are only slightly in frame it would be 100+35+70(half body, bust(50% off), bust).
  • Payments through wise only.

Edits: Any major edits must be specified before lineart is started, minor edits can be made at any stage.

Cancellations and Refunds: 50% refund if only sketch is done. No refunds after base color has been filled in.

Commissioned artwork is for your personal use and must not be resold or used for commercial purposes unless given explicit permission to do so.
